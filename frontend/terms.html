Platform rules

Terms & Conditions

These terms explain the basic rules for using TshongMart as a buyer, seller, or visitor.

1. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for providing accurate account, contact, and product information. False or misleading listings may be removed by the admin.

2. Product Listings

Sellers should upload clear details about the automotive product, including condition, price, location, and contact information. TshongMart may review or remove listings that appear suspicious.

3. Buyer and Seller Communication

Buyers and sellers may communicate through external contact links such as WhatsApp or Telegram. Users should verify product details before making any payment or agreement.

4. Payments

In the first version, TshongMart does not directly process real payments. Payment confirmation features are for testing and pitching purposes until a secure payment integration is added.

5. Admin Support

The admin may intervene to manage listings, support users, and maintain a safer marketplace. However, users are still responsible for checking product authenticity before finalizing deals.

6. Future Updates

These terms may be updated as TshongMart develops into a more complete platform with backend, database, image upload, admin tools, and payment systems.
